# Journey review · 27 October 2026

Status: research and product-design proposal, based on source `a4479b5` and the user's review of the public 1.35 km preview. No runtime implementation, renderer migration, provider purchase or new experience direction is approved by this document. Lead owns the decision and subsequent work authorization.

## User feedback and current goal

The user finds the buildings and roads rigid and unrealistic, cannot independently recognize the six sights without indications, and finds the website's many links confusing and disruptive. Perceived realism, recognition and immersion acceptance are reopened. Earlier automated passes and bounded art reviews retain their recorded technical scope; they do not establish acceptance of the experience the user played.

The latest priority is credible ground-level place appearance and an immersive, continuous journey. The approved continuous 1–2 km / 5–8-sight goal remains the intended experience. Continuous free walking, looking around, EN/ZH guidance, practical route information and private return remain relevant requirements. The next action is research and a decision brief; production changes wait for a direction decision.

## One coherent journey, shared across the alternatives

1. **Entry:** Explore is the default. Present the selected place, a concise route description and one Start/Resume action. Search and other reviewed places open from Change place. Together remains an explicit planning mode with its actual service capability; it makes no shared-avatar claim. Direct pack links enter the same journey state.
2. **Travel:** after entry, the place occupies the main viewport. Use a compact header and a small action bar. Walking/camera instructions and day/night remain requirements; a representation that limits these needs an explicit direction decision. Show real queued destination, remaining graph distance and Stop while moving. A free walk has no compulsory stop list, tutorial completion or arrival card.
3. **Route:** open an in-place panel for the selected order, distance and Map view. Edit reveals reorder/add/remove and preferences. Recommendations stay previews until Choose. Closing the panel restores the scene; inspecting another place preserves the chosen itinerary. A route remains an intention, separate from walked progress.
4. **Place:** explicit inspection opens the selected place's concise detail, useful practical information and expandable Sources. The scene must carry recognizable form, materials and spatial context before its name is revealed. Optional place guidance assists discovery; it does not count as evidence of unaided recognition.
5. **Keep and return:** Keep this place saves the supported view and optional personal words. Saved opens a local collection in the same application. Returning restores location, view, presentation mode and chosen route; obsolete records remain preserved with their existing unavailable-state handling. Changing representation needs a versioned, explicit view mapping before migration.
6. **Practical handoff:** Route contains the deliberate Open in Google Maps action and all ordered route parts. State clearly that Google calculates directions. External reference links remain in Sources; credits remain accessible. Older walk, perspective and on-site companion URLs remain compatible and are grouped under Other experiences rather than appearing as simultaneous journey steps.

Desktop panels occupy a bounded side region; compact layouts use an accessible dismissible sheet with the scene retained above it. Keep Stop and the route-loading/retry state reachable. Panel input must not steer the camera. Opening a reading/editing panel pauses queued movement while retaining its goal and pose; an explicit Resume returns to movement. Closing a panel restores focus to its opener, and Escape closes the active panel before invoking the underlying camera-mode action. This is proposed behavior requiring implementation verification.

Illustrative EN/ZH labels: **Start exploring / 开始探索**, **Resume your walk / 继续漫步**, **Route / 路线**, **About this place / 地点详情**, **Saved places / 收藏地点**, **Change place / 更换地点**, **Sources / 资料来源**, **Other experiences / 其他体验**, **Open in Google Maps / 在 Google Maps 打开**. Full localized guidance and failure states remain required. Verified Japanese world signs and original proper names retain their source meaning.

Experience evidence for the comparison and any later prototype:

- **Recognition:** review actual world views with product labels, pins and captions hidden. Record prior place familiarity. Familiar observers identify the place and its visible cues; unfamiliar observers match it to an unseen real-photo angle after exploration and explain the correspondence. Use actual geometry/material/spatial evidence, including lawful sourced world signs. Preserve confusion and assistance; invent no success rate or participants.
- **Immersion:** trace one continuous approach, deliberate looking, pause and return. Record every jump, loading stop, forced camera move, menu interruption and external navigation. Compare eye-height and third-person views on the same scene, including street scale, visibility, orientation and camera obstruction. Observe curiosity and sense of location. Capture quality, camera presentation and movement agency remain separate observations.
- **Usability:** a person enters/resumes, opens and dismisses Route/Place/Saved, keeps a view, returns and intentionally opens Maps in EN/ZH desktop and compact layouts. The main journey stays in one application; Back, Escape, focus, loading failures and private data have defined outcomes.
- **Quality and cost:** compare source-grounded sight cues, street-level surface detail, lighting coherence and capture defects. Record transferred bytes, preparation/review work, source-refresh obligations and provider/hosting charges with their evidence. Existing cold/warm, input/frame, Q03 and resource limits remain unchanged; software-host timing remains separately identified.

Decision exits: select only a direction supported by credible ground-level data and a demonstrated journey worth continuing. If none supplies both recognizable place and acceptable movement, report that result and the missing evidence before production. Technical checks, earlier composite art acceptance and a simplified interface alone cannot close the user's reopened recognition or immersion concern.
