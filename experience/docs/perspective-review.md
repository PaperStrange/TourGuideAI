# Sprint 2 · Review the experience

This is a formative comparison of the delivered formats. It does not yet establish
market demand, reciprocal connection or real-world travel benefit. The current
author is an explicitly labelled studio sample; a real contributor is a later test.

## Session

1. Record the participant's device, language, familiarity with Kyoto and prior use
   of this demo. Use a fresh browser profile for an initial assignment; an existing
   record resumes its format. Direct `?mode=walk` and `?mode=story` links select a
   format rather than randomize it. Alternate first-format order when comparing
   both, and record that order outside the application.
2. Invite the participant to explore without explaining which format we prefer.
   Let them decide whether to read, continue, save a view, write or share. Record
   assistance, load/control trouble, where they stop and what they say. A blank
   note or an early stop is a useful observation, not a task failure.
3. After the first format, collect its optional enjoyment score and explanation.
   The application retains one reflection; explicitly export it before trying the
   other format if both responses are needed. Do not reset private moments merely
   to manufacture a fresh-looking result. Switching marks the record as a crossover.
4. Try the equivalent content in the other format. Keep the same observations and
   note opportunities. Familiarity and extra walking time remain comparison limits.
5. If the participant chooses to share, preview one saved moment together and
   verify the inclusion checkbox. A recipient should open the link on a separate
   device or profile. Do not treat a generated link as evidence that someone read it.

## Neutral discussion prompts

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| What caught your attention? | 什么吸引了你的注意？ |
| What made you continue, or stop? | 什么让你想继续，或停下来？ |
| How would you describe this experience to a friend? | 你会怎样向朋友描述这段体验？ |
| What, if anything, felt different between the two formats? | 两种方式有什么不同的感受？如果没有，也可以直说。 |
| What, if anything, would you carry into a real visit? | 如果真的去那里，你会带着这次体验中的什么发现？如果没有，也可以直说。 |
| After opening a shared moment, what would you want to do next? | 打开别人分享的记录后，你接下来想做什么？ |

Do not introduce labels such as sandbox, AR/VR or AI agent before asking how the
participant describes it. Recognition, enjoyment, continuation, connection and
travel utility are separate observations. A hypothetical real visit is a stated
intention; only later field observation can establish actual transfer.

## Sprint review record

Keep individual observations and denominators visible. Separate first-use sessions
from crossovers, familiar visitors from unfamiliar ones, and device failures from
content reactions. No recruiting or personal information collection is automated.
Feedback is local unless the participant chooses to export and provide it.

Close with the largest supported friction, the smallest proposed change, measured
content/engineering/revision effort and a continue/revise/stop decision. Follow
[the phase gates](lean-iteration.md#phases-sprints-and-backlog) before committing to
city coverage. Do not convert a small convenience sample into a success percentage
or select whichever metric makes the prototype look strongest.
