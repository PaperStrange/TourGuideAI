The Kyoto and Ginza scenes combine sourced place facts with original, simplified 3D interpretations. The photographic references below guide façade shape, streetscape and materials; their pixels are not used as scene textures. Separate licensed environment photographs provide lighting and reflections. The photographers do not endorse this project.

Ginza geometry and building specifications银座几何与建筑规格

The Ginza pack retains the mapped Wako footprint and a connected sidewalk centerline from OpenStreetMap. Its 4 m walking corridor width, level ground and player clearance are authored simulation settings. They do not establish current pavement width, obstructions or access.

银座区域保留了 OpenStreetMap 中的和光建筑轮廓与连续人行道中心线。4 米行走范围宽度、水平地面与角色活动边距为模拟场景的设计参数，不代表当前人行道宽度、障碍或通行条件。

SEIKO HOUSE building specifications report 30.30 m to the rooftop, a 9.09 m clock-tower rise and 39.39 m from ground to tower top; the lightning rod is a separate 8 m quantity. The operator identifies polished Mannari stone on floors 1–2 and a tapped granite finish above. Individual floor elevations, bay spacing, hidden surfaces and material colours include authored interpretation.

SEIKO HOUSE 建筑规格记载屋顶距地面 30.30 米、钟楼高度 9.09 米、钟楼顶部距地面 39.39 米；避雷针长度另记为 8 米。运营方记载一至二层使用抛光万成石，三层及以上使用敲凿饰面的花岗岩。各层标高、窗组间距、不可见表面与材质颜色包含设计表达。
