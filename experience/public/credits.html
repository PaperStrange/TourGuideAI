Sources & credits资料来源与署名

The street combines sourced place facts with an original, simplified 3D interpretation. The four architecture photographs below guide façade shape and materials; their pixels are not used as scene textures. Separate licensed environment photographs provide lighting and reflections. The photographers do not endorse this project.

这条街结合了有来源的地点事实与原创的简化三维表达。以下四张建筑照片为立面造型和材质提供视觉参考，其像素未用作场景贴图；另行授权的环境照片用于光照与反射。摄影作者未为本项目背书。

Day & night environments日间与夜间环境光

Lighting and reflections use two photographic environment maps from Poly Haven, under CC0 1.0 Universal. They were captured in London and Berlin and provide an artistic lighting reference. They are not shown as a panorama background and do not describe Kyoto's actual lighting or surroundings.

光照与反射使用两张来自 Poly Haven、遵循 CC0 1.0 Universal 的摄影环境图。它们拍摄于伦敦和柏林，用于艺术化光照参考，不作为全景背景展示，也不代表京都的实际光照或周边环境。

World exploration世界探索

World maps use OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and © OpenStreetMap contributors. Photon supplies submitted place-name searches. Nearby sightseeing references come from Wikipedia contributors · CC BY-SA; each returned place links to its source article. Suggested visiting orders are calculated by TourGuideAI. Map lines indicate destination order; Google Maps computes walking directions externally.

世界地图使用 OpenFreeMap、OpenMapTiles 与 OpenStreetMap 数据。Photon 提供地点名称搜索，周边景点参考来源为 Wikipedia contributors · CC BY-SA，每个地点均附有来源链接。TourGuideAI 根据类别与距离建议游览顺序；地图连线表示地点顺序，实际步行导航由 Google Maps 计算。

The homepage uses MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3-Clause) and eventsource-parser (MIT).

首页使用 MapLibre GL JS（BSD-3-Clause）与 eventsource-parser（MIT）。

Map data & place facts地图数据与地点事实

© OpenStreetMap contributors · Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0

Building spans and crossing positions use OpenStreetMap data. Materials, model heights, interaction points and unassigned doors include authored approximations. Reaching a game interaction point does not establish a real visit or verify place facts.

建筑沿街范围与横道位置使用 OpenStreetMap 数据。材质、模型高度、互动位置和未匹配租户的门包含设计近似值。到达游戏互动点不等于现实到访或事实核验。

Expanded intersection geometry and source hashes扩展路口几何与来源校验值

The visual context includes mapped building footprints, road and sidewalk centrelines, hedges and a tree. Display widths and heights are interpreted; additional surroundings do not establish walkable access or new venue encounters.

扩展场景使用地图中的建筑轮廓、道路与人行道中心线、绿篱和树木位置。展示宽度与高度包含设计近似值；画面里的周边环境不代表新增可步行路线或地点体验。

Service and access details are paraphrased from the operators linked inside each encounter.

服务与交通信息依据各个互动中列出的运营方资料转述。
