## Held-out evidence procedure

Reserve the new photographs from geometry authoring, texture selection, splat
training and visual tuning. Their present use is identity/coverage qualification.
The verification script checks source filenames and hashes against existing
authoring registries. This check cannot establish independent capture authorship
or calibrated accuracy. A trial reviewer must record any later exposure of these
references to asset authors and commission fresh views where independence has
been lost. All trial participants use the matched cameras and acceptance criteria
defined in the sprint experiments; photographic reference availability alone does
not qualify a reconstruction.
