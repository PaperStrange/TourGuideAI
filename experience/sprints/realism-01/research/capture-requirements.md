## Capture and handover checklist

- Bind every original to capture time, author/rightsholder, location/pose, camera/lens settings, license or written permission and SHA-256. Record publication dates separately from capture dates.
- Include eye-level views near 1.7 m, close frontage views at 2–5 m where physically accessible, side/corner/reverse views, covered passages and bridge/river transitions. Record inaccessible views rather than inventing surfaces.
- Reserve independent photographs for evaluation before training or texture selection. Preserve overlap, consistent exposure and reconstruction logs; report subjects or reflections that prevent faithful reconstruction.
- Supply evidence of rights to process the same originals into meshes and splats, edit/repair, host publicly, retain originals, refresh and change vendors. Identify third-party artwork, signage and people handling in delivered material.
- Deliver measured ground/collision references independently of visual detail levels. Keep surveyed elevations and authored repairs explicitly labeled.
- Test receipt hashes, spatial coverage, both representations' source correspondence, held-out views and normal walking perspectives. Accept the capture package only when remaining gaps and remedies are documented.

## Use of existing sources

Reuse reviewed CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC0 material within its recorded terms. Source license, photographer credit, modification notice and any applicable ShareAlike obligations accompany the derived asset. Google imagery is excluded from exported mesh/texture derivation under the retained policy review. Any provider-dependent live renderer needs a separately verified distribution, billing and regional assessment. Software licenses alone provide no rights to input imagery.
