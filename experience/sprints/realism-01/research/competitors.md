## Use in this sprint

Compare architectural identity, close-range detail, place selection, movement
continuity and the usefulness of keeping a view. These references inform the
matched Kyoto trials. Renderer selection still requires the same licensed source
scene, camera path and physical-device conditions for each candidate. The full
1.35 km route and all six sights remain the delivery objective.

All third-party images are attributed research references. Permission for product
asset reuse has not been established. YouTube yielded no usable frame and
Instagram access failed in the original session; official publisher players
provided the retained video stills. No new competitor sessions were performed
during this organizational sprint.
