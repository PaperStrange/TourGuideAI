## Rendering decision

The current public reference is Pages commit
`3f1d3e6f578877f8d7fdd908e3f19a9b3eb658f3`, produced from runtime commit
`b4b140d2c48b7e185e296272f9fb141588caca95`. Its package manifest SHA-256 is
`6673bc5de654b71ba9e0fc5a799d72cb24af3654264aa1b3841c1236ae8223b5`.

Keep the captured lighting, camera calibration, SH2 streamed representation,
display resolution and detail limits. The official implementation uses fixed
65,536-splat pages. A compression candidate must preserve the decoded scene and
page organization. HTTP fragments cannot establish an ordinary-section budget
pass. First partial visibility and full-detail readiness retain separate clocks.

Each candidate requires exact input/output identities, converter revision and
options, complete size measurements, official decode comparison and matched
browser evidence. Only an evidenced improvement enters the public review.
The existing [loading release](../../evidence/realism-01/review-loading/README.md)
remains the comparison baseline.

## Connector decision

The Panoramax lead contains imagery inside the route rectangle. Rectangle
membership is an intake result. A usable connector additionally requires
overlapping views of the same surfaces, recoverable camera poses/calibration,
source rights, measured scale, continuous pedestrian ground and correspondence
with the route and recognizable sights. Independent lighting and holdout images
remain required by the [acceptance matrix](acceptance.md).

New imagery can support a bounded reconstruction trial after its rights and
overlap are established. Source limitations must stay visible in the candidate
record. Supplier outreach, purchases and paid capture remain outside this period.

## Delivery horizon

The previous estimate remains provisional: 1–2 active weeks for loading/device
profiling and connector qualification; 2–3 active weeks for a walking proof after
usable inputs are ready; 6–10 active weeks total after source qualification for
the complete route. These allowances exclude source waiting time and depend on
physical-device and participant availability. They do not establish a calendar
deadline or city-wide game scope.
