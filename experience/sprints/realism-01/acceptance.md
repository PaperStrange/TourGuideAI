# Milestone acceptance matrix

5 November 2026 · QA and product jointly own acceptance. [Benchmark V1](benchmark.md)
defines the frozen conditions and measurements; the [lesson register](lessons.md)
links the user's experience requirements. Production gates remain open until their
own complete evidence exists.

| Gate | Method / required result | Current evidence |

Recruit 32 fresh, unfamiliar, non-author participants **per compared product**, with
eight each in EN/day, ZH/day, EN/night and ZH/night. Every first exposure belongs to
one product/cohort. Physical devices are the declared Windows 11 / Iris Xe / 16 GB /
Chrome laptop and MacBook Air M1 / 8 GB / Safari; exact identities and availability
must be recorded before acceptance. These dependencies remain unarranged.

Historical [candidate-13 measurements](../../evidence/neighborhood-20261024/performance/candidate-13/README.md)
retain their original software-rendering and starting-segment scope. New protocol
conditions require new candidate runs. Independent full-route human results follow
comparable candidate builds and precede the final representation decision.
